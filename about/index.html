About the Sages

The Sages are an AI, trained on the Tarot and I Ching traditions. The cast is chance; the meanings are the traditions'; the Sages help you read them.

The cast is real chance: nothing in DuoSages chooses your cards or coins. The meanings come from the Tarot and I Ching traditions themselves. The Sages help you read what was cast in the light of your question.

They keep what you share in each conversation, and a few notes you can see and edit under What the Sages notice. Your words go to trusted AI service providers to write the replies.

DuoSages offers perspective for reflection. It does not make predictions or give medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and the decision is always yours.

If something seems wrong. Press and hold anything the Sages write to report it, or use "Report this reading" at the foot of a reading. Every report is read.
